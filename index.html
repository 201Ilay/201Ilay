Stakes & settings

Small bet is a low-risk outside bet (red/black, odd/even, 1–18/19–36, or a dozen) using your small stake. Big bet spreads your big stake over a group of numbers that sit next to each other on the wheel and have been hitting most, paid 35 to 1 each.

Every spin is independent. Past numbers do not change the odds of the next one, and the house edge (2.7% on a single-zero wheel, 5.26% with 00) applies to every bet here. These picks follow the patterns in the spins you entered. Set a loss limit before you play.
